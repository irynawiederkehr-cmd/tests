Who I am and why I understand you

Hello! My name is Iryna

I’m a transformational coach, neurocoach and mentor, the author of the “Steps. Your path in a new country” method. I live in Switzerland and know first-hand what it’s like to start life over in a foreign country.

That is why you are always at the centre of our work. Your life, your strength and your wellbeing.

We go deeper than advice

We won’t stop at a plan on paper. We will gently go down to where your habitual reactions, fears and old rules live, the ones that no longer work here. When something changes inside you, solutions on the outside start to appear by themselves.

You stop blaming yourself

I understand how your brain reacts to change and an unfamiliar environment. Why in a new country your energy runs out so fast and a simple letter from an authority feels like a mountain. This is not about weakness. This understanding alone lifts a lot of needless guilt off your shoulders.

Your attention comes back to you

I work with your thinking and the focus of your attention. Where your attention goes, your energy goes too. Together we will find the thoughts that go round in circles and learn to shift your gaze from “why isn’t it working for me” to “what can I do for myself today”. Then your mind grows quieter, and there is room for your own decisions.

Everything I know about this path I have gathered into the “Steps. Your path in a new country” method. These are eight steps by which life after a move comes back together. You don’t need to hide the cracks along the way. In time they become golden seams, and your experience of moving becomes your strength.

I don’t rush you or pull you by the hand. I walk beside you — at your pace and for your sake.Iryna

How I work

What our work rests on

I don’t give advice, and I don’t know better than you what is right. Right is whatever suits you. My job is to ask the questions you don’t ask yourself and to help you hear your own answers.

The “Steps. Your path in a new country” method

This is my own method. Life in a new country comes back together over eight steps, from “The broken bowl”, when there is no energy and nothing to lean on, to “Here and there — one life”. The lower steps hold up the higher ones. While sleep and a roof over your head are missing, it’s too early to look for your calling, and that isn’t weakness but the order in which everything is built.

Each step has three stages. Finding my feet — I’m getting my bearings and need information and something to lean on. Standing firm — it’s working for me. Reaching higher — it starts to feel too tight, and I look for room to grow. Most often people come to me when “reaching higher” turns into “I want to, but I don’t do it”. That is exactly where our work begins.

Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask

How is coaching different from psychotherapy?

Therapy more often works with the past and with what hurts. Coaching works with the present and with where you want to go. I don’t treat or diagnose; I help you find your own solution and get to action.

What is the “Steps. Your path in a new country” method?

It is my own method. It describes life after a move as eight steps, from “The broken bowl” to “Here and there — one life”, with three stages on each. A step is not a grade, and the first is no worse than the eighth. After a big change you may slip down a step, and that’s normal. The method helps you see where you are now and which next step is within your reach. You can see all the steps on the page “Your path in a new country”.

How many sessions do I need?

For a specific question, one to three are often enough. For changes on the level of “who do I want to be here”, three months or more. I’ll tell you more precisely after we meet.

I have nothing to say, I can’t even put into words what I want to work on

That’s a normal start, not an obstacle. Putting it into words is part of my job. It’s enough to come with the phrase “something’s not right”.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Everything that happens in a session stays between us — a coach’s professional ethics require it, and I follow them.

I don’t live in Switzerland. Does it work?

Yes, all sessions take place online. We’ll take the time difference into account when booking.

And what if it doesn’t click after the first session?

Then we don’t continue — and that’s a perfectly normal outcome. The 30-minute introductory meeting is there precisely to check this before you pay.

When coaching isn’t the right fit

Coaching works when you have the strength to move. There are times when you need a doctor or a psychotherapist more — and I’ll tell you so directly, because your wellbeing matters more.

  • An acute state, when getting through an ordinary day is hard
  • A recent loss or trauma that hasn’t been processed yet
  • A request like “diagnose me” or “prescribe me treatment”
  • Wanting someone else to change, not you
  • Expecting a ready-made plan and instructions — that’s what a consultant is for

If you’re having thoughts of not wanting to live, or you’re in danger, please contact a doctor or the emergency services in your country. There’s no shame in it. I’ll always tell you who to turn to.
