Anchor map

What holds you in a new country and what to strengthen first

After a move, much of what you leaned on stayed behind there. Your familiar home, your language, your work, people who had known you for many years. Here life has to be put back together, from old and new, outside and inside — like a bowl mended with gold. This map will help you see what already holds you, what wobbles and which anchor to strengthen first. With one small step.

Point A · where you start

It feels as if there is nothing to hold on to. Your energy is low, everything wobbles, and it’s unclear where to begin.

Point B · what you leave with

A map of the eight anchors on one page, in your own words. You see what already holds you, where it’s empty and which anchor to strengthen first. And you have one small step for this week.

The key insight

You have more anchors than it seems. And it’s worth starting not with the loudest trouble or the biggest dream, but with the lowest anchor that wobbles. Everything holds from the bottom: the lower steps hold up the higher ones.
